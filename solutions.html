01 · Membrane Systems

Industrial Reverse Osmosis (BWRO & SWRO)

Skid-mounted brackish and seawater reverse osmosis trains built with structural SS304/SS316L frames, ASME 1200 PSI fiberglass pressure vessels, and Grundfos or Danfoss high-pressure duplex pumps.

Capacity 50–5,000 m³/d
Recovery 75% – 85%
Rejection > 99.5% TDS
Controls Siemens PLC
Orbital TIG Welding Sanitary and high-pressure manifold welds inspected via non-destructive testing (NDT) with weld maps.
Integrated CIP Skid Dedicated clean-in-place pumping loop with pH neutralization, micron cartridge guard, and thermal heating.
1.5x Hydrostatic FAT Full working-pressure hydraulic test and wet conductivity run documented prior to export crating.
