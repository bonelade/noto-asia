01 · Maritime Port Hub

Port of Tanjung Priok, Jakarta (IDTPP)

Located 150 km from our West Java fabrication bay via direct expressways, Tanjung Priok serves as our international maritime gateway for FCL, Flat Rack, and breakbulk heavy skid shipments with Maersk, MSC, Evergreen, and ONE.

  • —Direct feeder loops to Singapore (SGSIN) & Port Klang (MYPKG) in 24–48 hours.
  • —Breakbulk & heavy-lift crane coordination for oversized DAF tanks and skids.
  • —Bonded warehouse staging for multi-phase EPC project dispatches.
