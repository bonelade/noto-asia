Our Story

From Specialized Water Chemistry to an Integrated B2B Engineering Enterprise

Founded in Bandung, Indonesia in 1995, PT Noto Water Envirotech emerged from a practical conviction: industrial water challenges cannot be solved with off-the-shelf catalog equipment. Reliable uptime requires ionic spectrometry, precise hydraulic modeling, and unyielding fabrication quality.

Over three decades, we have commissioned more than 1,200 treatment plants spanning municipal drinking water stations, high-COD textile recovery lines, pharmaceutical purified loops, and remote mining camps.

Our tripartite organization aligns R&D water chemists, ASME-certified orbital welders, and international export officers under a single ISO 9001:2015 management framework.
