PT Noto Water Envirotech combines 28+ years of field water engineering with state-of-the-art analytical research, heavy industrial skid fabrication, and global turnkey logistics.

From Specialized Water Chemistry to a Global Engineering Powerhouse

Founded in Bandung, Indonesia in 1995, PT Noto Water Envirotech was born from a fundamental realization: industrial water challenges cannot be solved with cookie-cutter machinery. True efficiency requires deep chemical spectrometry, exact hydraulic engineering, and unyielding fabrication precision.

Over the past three decades, we have commissioned over 1,200 systems across municipal drinking water plants, heavy textile effluent recovery facilities, food & beverage production lines, and mining camps.

Our tripartite operational structure brings together R&D scientists, ASME-certified mechanical welders, and international logistics directors under a unified quality management framework certified to ISO 9001:2015.
